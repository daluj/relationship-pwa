# Growth and networking ideas (not scheduled)

Suggested alongside Phases 10–12; promote to a phase during replanning.

- **Inner circle**: a special group with a target size and meeting cadence, plus a "what I learn from them" note per person.
- **Give and ask ledger**: intros, favors and referrals given and received; extends gifts/debts.
- **Leverage map**: tag what each contact could offer or need (money, time, expertise, access) with a filterable view. Phase 11 starts this with free-text offer/need notes.
- **Learning log**: books, courses and lessons, linked to goals and to the people who recommended them.
- **Daily vision/focus card**: one goal and one next action on the Home screen each day (partly covered by the Phase 10 Today card).

Constraints: no scoring or ranking of people (mission §1.2, D-069); neutral wording; no network calls.
